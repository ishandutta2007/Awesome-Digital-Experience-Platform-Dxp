# Awesome-Digital-Experience-Platform-Dxp

## Top Digital Experience Platform (DXP) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Content Management, Personalization & Omnichannel Experience Delivery*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Experience Platforms (DXP)**. These tools enable organizations to build, manage, and deliver personalized digital experiences across web, mobile, and other channels — combining content management, customer data, personalization, and analytics.



**Examples** include Salesforce Digital Experience Platform, Adobe Experience Manager, Sitecore, Optimizely One, Acquia DXP, Bloomreach, Magnolia CMS, Liferay DXP, Contentful, and Storyblok (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, headless architectures, and open experience management — ideal for enterprises, publishers, and developers seeking transparent, vendor-independent DXP solutions.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Digital Experience Platform](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  

  Experience Cloud-powered DXP for building communities, portals, help centers, and websites. Features Lightning Web Components, Einstein AI personalization, and deep CRM integration. Requires enabling Digital Experiences in setup; once enabled, it cannot be disabled .



- **[Adobe Experience Manager](https://business.adobe.com/products/experience-manager/sites/aem-sites.html)**  

  Enterprise DXP with two distinct offerings: AEM 6.5 LTS (self-hosted or Adobe-managed, highly customizable, perpetual licensing available) and AEM as a Cloud Service (subscription-only, built-in CDN, auto-scaling). Supports Java, HTL, JSP, and Dispatcher customization .



- **[Sitecore](https://www.sitecore.com/)**  

  Enterprise DXP with personalization, analytics, and marketing automation. Note: Sitecore XP mainstream support ends in 2027, creating migration urgency for many organizations .



- **[Optimizely One](https://www.optimizely.com/)**  

  Composable DXP with CMS, experimentation, and personalization. Q1 2026 shipped Opal agents, CMS13, GEO agents for AI search, and program-aware experimentation . Limitless 1:1 personalization enables AI agents to build microsites for each target buyer .



- **[Acquia DXP](https://www.acquia.com/)**  

  Open DXP built on Drupal. Features content governance, 300+ customer data integrations, identity resolution for 360° customer view, predictive marketing with ML models, and no-code/low-code experience building. Customers report 50% development time reduction and $15M saved over three years .



- **[Bloomreach](https://www.bloomreach.com/)**  

  Commerce experience platform combining search, content management, and marketing automation. Indexes catalogs 80% faster than alternatives with sub-50ms response times. Enterprise pricing based on customer volume, catalog size, and event execution .



- **[Magnolia CMS](https://www.magnolia-cms.com/)**  

  Single-vendor commercial open-source CMS. Community version available with enterprise version for additional features like mobile support. Uses Confluence for its own wiki (commercial engine) .



- **[Liferay DXP](https://www.liferay.com/)**  

  Enterprise DXP with 2026.Q3 release adding Liferay Data Platform, Content Marketing Platform, Digital Sales Room, and AI Hub out of beta . Free Tier available (12-month activation key) with core capabilities including clustering up to three nodes . All installations since 2026.Q1 require XML activation keys .



- **[Contentful](https://www.contentful.com/)**  

  API-first headless CMS for digital teams. Features flexible content modeling, GraphQL/REST APIs, and multi-channel content delivery at scale. Migration to open-source alternatives like Strapi is documented with tools like strapi_lift .



- **[Storyblok](https://www.storyblok.com/)**  

  Headless CMS with visual editor for component-based content. Supports field-level, folder-level, and Space-level localization strategies. Spaces function as independent content repositories with their own users, roles, and workflows .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Liferay DXP](https://github.com/liferay/liferay-portal)**  

  Enterprise open-source DXP (LGPL-2.1) with the same core platform as Enterprise Subscription. Free Tier provides content management, sites, pages, web experiences, headless APIs, and workflow. Supports MariaDB, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle, IBM DB2, and Microsoft SQL Server. Cloud Native includes GA support for GCP Ready and Kubernetes Ready .



- **[Magnolia CMS Community](https://github.com/magnolia-cms)**  

  Community edition of Magnolia CMS (single-vendor commercial open source). Provides enterprise content management capabilities with community support. Enterprise version required for features like mobile support .



- **[Bloomreach Experience CMS](https://github.com/bloomreach/brxm)**  

  Apache-licensed community edition of Bloomreach Experience CMS. 26 GitHub stars. The commercial Bloomreach platform adds search, personalization, and marketing automation on top of the core CMS .



- **[Apache Unomi](https://github.com/apache/unomi)**  

  Open-source customer data platform (Apache-2.0) for managing customers, leads, and visitors data. Features real-time event processing, profile management, rules engine for personalization, and REST API. GDPR-compliant with privacy features. Can integrate with Salesforce CRM via connector .



- **[Umbraco](https://github.com/umbraco/Umbraco-CMS)**  

  Open-source .NET CMS (MIT) positioned as a lower-cost Sitecore alternative. Organizations report 60-70% reduction in total cost across licensing, hosting, and development. Projects go live in approximately four months. API-first with integrations for commerce, DAM, CRM, and analytics .



- **[ApostropheCMS](https://github.com/apostrophecms/apostrophe)**  

  Open-source CMS (MIT) with in-context editing and advanced permissions. Supports unlimited core locales without SaaS subscription limits. Assembly provides one shared CMS application with site provisioning and isolation for multiple sites .



- **[Strapi](https://github.com/strapi/strapi)**  

  Leading open-source headless CMS (MIT). 65k+ GitHub stars. 100% JavaScript/TypeScript, fully customizable, and self-hosted. Features REST and GraphQL APIs, role-based access control. Migration tools available from Contentful .



- **[Drupal](https://github.com/drupal/drupal)**  

  Enterprise-grade open-source CMS (GPL-2.0). Used by 8.2% of top 10,000 sites. Robust content modeling, multilingual support, and accessibility compliance. Foundation for Acquia DXP .



- **[WordPress](https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress)**  

  World's most popular CMS (GPL-2.0), powering 55.8% of top 10,000 sites. Themes, plugins, and mature ecosystem. Available self-hosted or via managed platforms like Pantheon .



- **[Tiki Wiki CMS Groupware](https://github.com/tiki/tiki)**  

  Community open-source CMS (LGPL-3.0) with the most built-in features among FLOSS web applications. Easier hosting requirements (shared hosting compatible). All features included as open source, unlike Magnolia which requires Enterprise Edition for some capabilities .



- **[Silverstripe CMS](https://github.com/silverstripe/silverstripe-cms)**  

  Open-source headless CMS (BSD-3-Clause) for building and managing content for applications. Features activity dashboard for developers .



- **[Crafter CMS](https://github.com/craftercms/craftercms)**  

  Open-source DXP (GPL-3.0) with Git-based content management, DevContentOps, and personalization. Crafter Studio provides authoring environment .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[CoreMedia](https://www.coremedia.com/)** — Composable DXP with hybrid headless CMS (license varies), real-time data integration, cloud or on-premise deployment .

- **[Brightspot](https://www.brightspot.com/)** — Content management solution for global brands with customizable interface and workflow support .

- **[Contentstack](https://www.contentstack.com/)** — Headless CMS with CDN-backed APIs for personalized content delivery .

- **[Squiz DXP](https://www.squiz.net/)** — Platform for intelligent digital experiences with personalization and journey optimization .

- Community **n8n** workflows for DXP data orchestration and personalization automation.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Liferay DXP** or **Umbraco** as the core DXP with **Strapi** or **Drupal** for headless content management, **Apache Unomi** for customer data and personalization, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade digital experience platform.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, backup strategies, and ongoing maintenance. Liferay DXP requires activation keys for all installations since 2026.Q1 . Migration from proprietary platforms involves significant planning and potential data migration effort.
